The problem

A quarter of COD orders travel out and back for nothing

The brand pays shipping both ways, repacks the product, and has stock stuck in transit for one to two weeks with no sale at the end.

60–65%of Indian e-commerce orders are cash on delivery
~26%of COD orders end in RTO, up to ~35% in some cities
<3%RTO rate on prepaid orders, about a tenth of COD
₹180–240logistics cost of a single RTO, before lost sales

Sources: ET Prime 2024 and Shipway FY25 via Razorpay and Amazon Shipping. Bad addresses cause 18–24% of RTOs (bepragma.ai 2025). Acting works: GoKwik reports ₹130 Cr of RTO losses saved for brands in 2023 (CXOtoday).

A brand shipping 1,000 COD orders a day at 26% RTO loses about 260 orders × ₹200 = ₹52,000 a day, roughly ₹1.9 Cr a year in logistics alone.
Who it is for

The ops head at a brand with a high RTO rate

D2C and product brands in fashion, footwear, beauty, home and electronics, selling into Tier-2 and Tier-3 India. Today they find out about a bad order when it comes back. RTO Shield gives them a queue of risky orders and an action for each, before the parcel leaves the warehouse. The shopper gets a short call or message in Hindi or English instead of a parcel they didn't want or couldn't receive.

How it works

Score, pick the action, act, measure

1 · SCORE

Every new order

Delivery probability from pincode history, payment mode, customer history, address quality and order pattern.

2 · EXPLAIN

Reasons, not just a number

Each order carries reason codes such as WEAK_ADDRESS or PAST_RTO.

3 · ACT

Rules pick the action

Probability band plus reason decides: Gemini call, prepaid or token nudge, address fix, or ship.

4 · MEASURE

Impact vs control

5% of risky orders get no action. Their RTO rate is the baseline the dashboard compares against.

Actions

The action follows the reason

A discount does nothing for a wrong address, and a clean address does nothing for a buyer who no longer wants the product.

Payment levers for COD

Turn cash on delivery into money upfront

Prepaid orders return under 3% of the time against about 26% for COD, so getting some money upfront is the strongest single lever. Which one we use depends on the customer.

MEDIUM-RISK COD

Convert to prepaid

Offer a small discount (for example 5%) to pay the full amount now. The order then behaves like a prepaid order.

HIGH-RTO CUSTOMER ON COD

Collect a token amount

Ask for a small part-payment (for example ₹99–199) before dispatch, with the rest on delivery. If it isn't paid, the order is held instead of shipped.

Rules screen

The ops team sets the bands

Rules live on the front end as probability bands, so a brand can tune them to its own margins. Default bands:

Below 40%Gemini voice call (COD or prepaid), plus address fix on WEAK_ADDRESS. COD with past RTOs also gets a token-amount request.
40–65%COD: WhatsApp nudge to pay in full now for a small discount. Prepaid: address check only.
Above 65%Ship as normal.
Impact dashboard

Proof against a control group, not a status board

  • Control group: 5% of risky orders, picked by order ID, get no action. Their RTO rate shows what would have happened.
  • Headline: RTO % treated vs control, orders saved, ₹ saved (orders saved × ~₹200), COD converted to prepaid, token amounts collected.
  • Breakdowns: results per action, call outcomes and sentiment, RTO by pincode tier.
  • Data: outcomes are simulated on synthetic orders, and we say so in the demo.
